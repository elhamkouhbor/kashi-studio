# Kashi Studio reference catalogue of Iranian tilework (کاشی)

A documented reference set for the Kashi Studio pattern families. 45 museum objects and 11 architectural sites. Machine-readable version: `catalogue.json`. Images: `../data/references/images/`.

## Method

- Museum records come only from the Metropolitan Museum of Art collection API (v1.1 search, v1 objects) and the Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access API, fetched in November 2026. Title, accession number, place, date and medium are copied from the API without change, including hedges such as 'probably' and 'attributed to'.
- The Met API returns an empty period/dynasty field for these objects. The period label in each Met record is therefore a cataloguer assignment made from the stated date and is marked as such. Cleveland periods are copied as given.
- Images were saved only when the source states public domain or CC0 (Met isPublicDomain true, Cleveland CC0), or for sites when Wikimedia Commons shows CC0, public domain or CC BY. CC BY-SA and unclear files were skipped. Met images were taken from the Met's own CC0 uploads on Wikimedia Commons because the Met image server refused automated requests. Images are resized to at most 900 px on the long side, JPEG quality 82.
- Each downloaded image was viewed. Notes separate what is observed (Observed) from what it suggests for Kashi Studio (Interpretation). Colour hex values are visual approximations from photographs and are affected by lighting and lustre sheen.
- Site descriptions come from English Wikipedia, used as a last resort because the UNESCO World Heritage pages blocked automated access. Each site record states this. Ornament is recorded only as the article states it, with photo observations marked separately.

### Panel Composed with Tiles in Shape of Eight-pointed Stars and Crosses  
`met-kashan-star-cross-panel`

**Institution:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York  
**Accession:** 41.165.22  
**Origin:** Made in probably Kashan, Iran  
**Date:** 1260–70  
**Period:** Ilkhanid [cataloguer, from the date 1260–70. Met API period field is empty]  
**Technique (as given):** "Stonepaste; luster-painted on opaque white glaze under transparent glaze"

![Panel Composed with Tiles in Shape of Eight-pointed Stars and Crosses](../data/references/images/met-kashan-star-cross-panel.jpg)

- **Families:** lustre, girih گره, medallion, calligraphy
- **Elements:** eight-pointed star tiles, cross tiles, inscription borders on stars, palmettes, teardrop medallions, birds and floral fills
- **Geometry:** Classic star-and-cross tessellation: regular 8-pointed stars (two overlapping squares) alternating with four-armed cross tiles whose arm ends are pointed. The crosses fill exactly the gaps between stars
- **Symmetry:** Tiling outline is p4m (4-fold centres at star centres and cross centres, mirror lines along rows and diagonals). Individual tile fills differ, so the decorated panel has no strict symmetry
- **Colours:** white glaze `#EDE8E0`, dark lustre brown `#6B3A22`, pale lustre tan `#C9A27A`, ruby-toned lustre `#9A5A4A`
- **Notes:** Observed: about 17 stars and the crosses between them reassembled into a panel. Each star has its own fill (palmette medallions, birds, floral rosettes, a large teardrop medallion) with a white script border. The crosses are mostly speckled lustre with sparse reserved designs. Interpretation: the single best reference here for the star-and-cross layout and tile proportions, and a reminder that historic panels varied the fill per tile.
- **Uncertainty:** Met: 'Made in probably Kashan'. Whether this grouping reflects an original wall arrangement is not stated in the API record.
- **Image rights:** Public domain (Met Open Access, CC0). Credit (as given): "The Metropolitan Museum of Art, H.O. Havemeyer Collection, Gift of Horace Havemeyer, 1941. Image file obtained via Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Panel_Composed_with_Tiles_in_Shape_of_Eight-pointed_Stars_and_Crosses_MET_DP251471.jpg), licence CC0"
